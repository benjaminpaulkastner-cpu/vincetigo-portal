Privacy Notice

Effective October 13, 2026

Who we are

Vincetigo Property Management LLC (“Vincetigo,” “we,” or “us”) operates this property management, leasing, and resident portal in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. This notice describes the information handled through this website and related property-management communications.

Information and how it is used

  • Contact and leasing inquiries: If you contact us by email or provide details through an inquiry or application flow, we may handle your name, email address, phone number, requested property, move-in preferences, and message to respond, coordinate leasing, and administer a tenancy. Do not send sensitive information by ordinary email.
  • Applications and documents: The application shown on this site is a demonstration. It does not transmit or retain the form details or uploaded files; do not enter real financial or identity information. Any live application process will identify its collection method, screening providers, required documents, and applicable fee before submission.
  • Maintenance requests and logs: The demonstration portal records a request’s category, priority, description/access notes, status, and date in local browser storage on the device used to submit it. These demo requests are not sent to dispatch and remain in that browser until its site data is cleared. Do not include passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information in a request. A production maintenance service may collect and retain request details and dispatch history to coordinate repairs, document work, and meet legal obligations.
  • Payments through Stripe: When a Stripe-hosted payment link is used, Stripe processes payment credentials on its own service. Vincetigo receives transaction and payment-status information needed to reconcile the payment, not your full card security code. Stripe’s own privacy terms apply to its processing.
  • Plaid and bank connections: Bank linking is unavailable on the static resident pages at this time. If a Plaid connection is offered in a live service, Plaid handles bank sign-in and may provide authorized account details or tokens for the requested payment or verification service. Do not send bank credentials to Vincetigo by email or enter them on a page that is not the Plaid flow. The portal does not store payment credentials or Plaid tokens in browser localStorage or sessionStorage.
  • Technical data: Hosting, security, and service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs to deliver and protect the website. The site also loads some third-party assets; those providers may receive standard connection data when your browser requests them.

Disclosure, retention, and choices

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that support hosting, payment processing, bank connectivity, maintenance, leasing, or legally required purposes, limited to the relevant service. We retain information only as reasonably needed for the stated purpose, property operations, and legal obligations. Demo maintenance records are kept in the browser as described above; clearing the browser’s site data removes them from that device.

For a privacy question or request concerning information handled by Vincetigo, email ben@vincetigo.com. Do not send account passwords, bank credentials, or payment card details in your request.

Housing decisions are made without discrimination based on any characteristic protected by applicable law. Reasonable accommodation requests may be sent to our support email.
